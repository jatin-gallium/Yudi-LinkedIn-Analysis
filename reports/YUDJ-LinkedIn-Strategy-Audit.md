## 1. Executive summary

**What is working**

- **Distribution:** Client-reported scale (~**1.37M impressions**, ~**402K members reached**) indicates the algorithm is willing to show UDJ’s content broadly.
- **Passive interest:** **~2,106 saves** (client-reported as **~37.8% of engagement**) suggests topics feel “worth keeping” even when they do not spark immediate conversation.

**What is broken**

- **Engagement efficiency:** ~**5,574 social engagements** on ~**1.37M impressions** ≈ **0.4%** engagement rate (client-reported). That is consistent with **broad relevance** and **weak scroll-stopping** rather than a small niche audience.
- **Action:** ~**1,437 link clicks** ≈ **0.1%** of impressions — classic **awareness without intent**.
- **Conversation:** **146 comments** (client-reported) vs high saves implies **bookmark-first** content, not **debate- or identity-first** content (which brands and sponsors often proxy as “community”).

**Strategic read (aligned with UDJ’s own hypothesis)**

- Peers who monetize with **one sharp POV** and **one or two offers** convert attention into **clarity**. UDJ’s feed may be winning **reach** by speaking to “everyone in tech + study abroad + visas,” while sponsors buy **defined audiences** and **repeatable narrative**.

**What we will do next**

- Run a **six-post** experiment designed to **raise comment/share rate** and **prove one ICP wedge** (not to maximize impressions).
- Parallel: complete **(data to paste)** inventories so this document becomes evidence-backed, not framework-only.

## 6. Six-post content strategy (concrete, execution-ready)

**Global rules for this sprint**

- **One ICP sentence** repeated across posts (pinned comment or P.S.).  
- **One primary CTA** per post (comment OR DM OR link — not all three).  
- **Hook rule:** first line must contain **specificity** (number, timeframe, contrarian claim, or named mistake).  
- **Engagement design:** end with a **binary choice**, **mistake hunt**, or **“what would you do?”** scenario.  
- **Measurement:** track **engagement rate per impression** and **comments per 1k impressions**, not raw impressions.

### Post 1 — Comment gravity (algorithm reset)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | Raise **comments**; accept lower saves. |
| **Format** | Short text (≤ 1,200 chars) + optional single image. |
| **Hook pattern** | “The most expensive mistake I see {ICP} make in {timeframe} is ___.” |
| **Body** | 3 bullets: mistake → why it happens → what to do instead (no link). |
| **CTA** | “Comment A if you’d fix it with ___. Comment B if you’d fix it with ___.” |
| **Proof** | One anonymized mini-case (numbers). |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 1 URL]` |

### Post 2 — Proof post (sponsor-adjacent)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | Show **receipts** (before/after, pipeline, offer conversion, student outcome). |
| **Format** | Document PDF or carousel (8–12 slides). Slide 1 = outcome number. |
| **Hook pattern** | “We had {metric}. We changed {one thing}. Here’s what moved.” |
| **CTA** | “If you want the checklist, comment **CHECKLIST**.” (Deliver via DM automation / manual DM.) |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 2 URL]` |

### Post 3 — Contrarian industry take (shareable)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | **Reposts** + debate (watch comment quality). |
| **Format** | Text; line breaks; no external link in main post. |
| **Hook pattern** | “Unpopular opinion: {common advice} is wrong for {ICP} because ___.” |
| **Guardrails** | Avoid punching down; target **ideas**, not people. |
| **CTA** | “Change my mind — what am I missing?” |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 3 URL]` |

### Post 4 — Deep tactical “one move” (save-worthy but comment-led)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | Earn saves **and** comments by forcing application. |
| **Format** | “One move Monday” template: single tactic with steps. |
| **Hook pattern** | “If you only had 60 minutes to ___, do these 5 steps.” |
| **CTA** | “Drop your profile niche + current obstacle; I’ll reply to 20 with the next step.” |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 4 URL]` |

### Post 5 — Creator-business transparency (monetization narrative)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | Train audience that UDJ sells **serious** outcomes (reduces “free advice” mismatch). |
| **Format** | Story arc: what was broken → what you changed → offer clarity. |
| **Hook pattern** | “We treated LinkedIn like a content machine. Revenue didn’t move until we ___.” |
| **CTA** | Single offer + who it is / isn’t for + **one** link in first comment. |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 5 URL]` |

### Post 6 — Peer contrast without naming-shaming (positioning)

| Field | Spec |
|-------|------|
| **Objective** | Encode **niche discipline** as a strategy, not jealousy. |
| **Format** | List post: “What the best {ICP} creators do differently.” |
| **Hook pattern** | “Smaller audiences can out-earn larger ones when ___.” |
| **CTA** | Poll in comments: “Which constraint is hardest for you: A) clarity B) proof C) CTA D) consistency” |
| **Paste live example after publish** | `[UDJ Post 6 URL]` |

**Cadence suggestion:** 3 posts/week for two weeks (Posts 1–6), same posting window, same CTA discipline.
