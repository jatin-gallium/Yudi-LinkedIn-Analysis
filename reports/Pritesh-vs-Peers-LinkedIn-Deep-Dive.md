# Pritesh Jagani (“Yudi J / UDJ”) vs peer creators — LinkedIn deep-dive report

**Audience:** Internal content strategy for UDJ LinkedIn.  
**Data sources:** Bulk caption exports in this repo (`AIshwaraya.md`, `Amney.md`, `Ruchi Bhatia.md`, `Sohan Sethi.md`, `Sundas Khalid.md`, `Venkata.md`, `Vishaka.md`, `Yudi J.md`).  
**Important name note:** `Yudi J.md` is an export of **Pritesh Jagani** (`linkedin.com/in/priteshjagani`). This report treats that account as **UDJ’s primary LinkedIn presence**.

---

## Executive summary

1. **Peers run a tighter “tech + career + AI” lane in the feed.** In the last ~six months of captured posts, peer content is **AI-heavy** (~56.8% of peer posts hit AI/ML keyword signals vs ~18.6% for Pritesh) and **visa-light** (~5.1% vs ~50.6%). Pritesh’s window is dominated by **immigration + jobseeker** language, which overlaps your ICP but **does not overlap sponsor narratives** (AI tooling, infra, cloud, ML platforms) as often.
2. **Pritesh’s distribution is “lottery-shaped.”** Median reactions on captured posts are **~43** vs **~300** for peers; yet **only ~3.7%** of his window posts reach **≥500 reactions** vs **~32%** for peers. That pattern is consistent with a few **viral narrative spikes** plus a long tail of posts that do not clear the bar for comment/repost velocity LinkedIn rewards.
3. **Repost gravity is weaker.** Peers average **~62.8 reposts per 1,000 reactions** vs Pritesh **~22.0** — peers package “save + share” frames (cheatsheets, tool stacks, contrarian AI takes) more often.
4. **Manifest Law is over-represented on Pritesh vs peers** (~27.5% of posts vs ~3.1%), while **Anthropic / Replit / NVIDIA-style density** is higher in the peer bundle. Sponsors buy **category adjacency**; a feed that reads like **immigration + general job advice** attracts different budgets than a feed that reads like **AI builders + data + cloud**.
5. **Format mix:** Pritesh is **~50% video** in-window vs peers **~9.2%** — video can inflate **impressions** while under-producing **comments per impression** if the story does not force a binary opinion early.
6. **Fix strategy:** tighten to **one wedge** (recommendation: **international software engineers navigating US hiring + AI leverage**, not “all of tech + all visas + all careers”), increase **AI-native builder proof**, add **2–3 document carousels/month**, and run **six engagement-first posts** mapped below with **real URLs** from peers + Pritesh.

**Client-reported dashboard context (from your brief):** ~1.37M impressions with ~0.4% engagement and heavy saves. That is **account-level** behavior. This report is **post-level text + public engagement snapshots** at export time; the two should be read together, not treated as identical metrics.

## 1. Methodology & limitations

- **Time window:** 2025-11-17 through 2026-05-17 (inclusive), anchored to latest peer export date **2026-05-17**.
- **Parsing:** Regex blocks `--- Post N ---` through the next post; engagement line parsed flexibly (some exports omit reposts or comments segments).
- **Topic & brand tags:** Heuristic keyword counts; posts can match **multiple** tags. “Manifest” also matches generic English “manifest”; interpret Manifest Law rows cautiously when the word appears outside immigration law context.
- **Outlier data:** Some peer rows show **comments > reactions** (rare); treat as **scrape timing** or LinkedIn display quirks, not as math to defend in court.
- **Ruchi Bhatia sample:** Only **47** posts fall in the six-month window — directional, not equal weight to 324-post windows.

## 9. What Pritesh is doing **wrong** vs peers (post corpus, not moral judgment)

1. **Lane width:** Topic tag mix shows **visa + jobs + creator** without the peer cohort’s **AI builder density**. Sponsors for AI tools want **builder identity**, not generic career advice with occasional AI lists.
2. **Baseline engagement:** Median reactions **~7× lower** than peer median — the feed is not “failing,” it is **inconsistent**.
3. **Shareability:** Reposts/1k reactions **~2.8× lower** — fewer **cheatsheet / resource** frames and fewer **binary debate** hooks.
4. **Documents:** Almost no **carousel / PDF** posts vs peers — missed **save + repost** mechanics.
5. **Brand graph:** Heavy **Manifest** association without matching **AI vendor** co-mentions reduces **perceived category ownership** for AI-native sponsors.
6. **Video skew:** High video share can help **reach** but hurts if thumbnails/first 2s are not **opinionated**; peers still win comments with **short image + sharp hook**.
